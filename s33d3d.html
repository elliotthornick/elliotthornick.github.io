S33D

S33D3D

Your setup, seeded anywhere.

In Testing

Free · Windows & Mac

The old way to set up a computer: visit a website, click through somebody else's fixed list of programs, and do it all again on the next machine. S33D3D flips it. You download the app once and configure your setup — the programs you actually chose — and that setup travels: onto your next machine, onto a USB stick, or out to a whole bench of computers at once.

Features

  • Two tabs, one catalog — a Users tab with the everyday essentials and a Techs tab with the technician's toolkit, each ranked by what people actually install
  • Your setup is yours — save your program list as a profile file you own; no account standing between you and your configuration
  • Channels — share a setup as a simple list anyone can import; a repair tech can publish one setup and apply it to every customer's machine
  • Add your own programs — any app, with its source on file, enters clearly labelled until it earns verification
  • USB packages — download your whole setup into one folder, carry it anywhere, and install offline; every file is hash-verified before it runs
  • Multi-machine seeding — one source computer serves your setup across the network to many machines at once, in controlled waves
  • Built against the stale standard — no fixed catalog, no clicking through lists, no configuration that lives on someone else's website
