GamR

Find your gaming group.

In Development

In development · premium tier planned

GamR is a location-based app for finding gaming groups near you — swipe to match with groups looking for players, not dates. Built for tabletop, TCG and video-game crews who need one more.

GamR is in development and coming soon. This page will grow as the app takes shape.

How it works

  • Groups, not dates — swipe to match with gaming groups near you that are looking for players
  • Built for crews — tabletop, TCG and video-game groups who need one more at the table
  • 18+ only
  • Safety by design — report and block tools, plus AI nudity scanning on images
  • Premium tier planned
