NetDeckR

Your whole Magic collection, in your pocket.

In Development

Free · NetDeckR Pro $3.99/mo (live prices)

NetDeckR tracks a Magic: The Gathering collection the way players actually own it — digital (Arena) and physical cards kept separate, never double-counted. Browse your collection with real Scryfall card art, build decks from what you actually own, and keep a shopping list for the physical hunt. The Drops tab tracks Secret Lair drops and new set releases with countdowns so you never miss a window.

Features

  • Digital and physical, kept separate — Arena cards and paper cards tracked side by side, never double-counted
  • 14,000+ card collection support — with full import of your existing collection
  • Real Scryfall art — tap any card for its full-size image; double-faced cards flip
  • Deck building from owned cards only — no more brewing decks you can't actually play
  • Drops tab — Secret Lair drops and new set releases, with countdowns
  • Pro unlocks live prices — card prices refreshed from Scryfall for your portfolio and shopping list; everything else is free
  • No ads in NetDeckR, ever
