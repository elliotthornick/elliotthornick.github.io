Support — Elliott Hornick apps

Need help with iControl Snap, RPR: Roll Punch Repeat, or Gassy Capys? Email: [email protected] — please include your app name, iPhone model, and iOS version.

Quick answers:

  • Restore a purchase: in the app, use “Restore Purchases” (App Store purchases are tied to your Apple ID).
  • Promo code not working: promo codes are redeemed in the App Store app (tap your profile picture, then “Redeem Gift Card or Code”), not inside the app. Once redeemed, open the app and the unlock is active.
  • Ads still showing after unlock: force-quit and reopen the app, then tap Restore Purchases.
