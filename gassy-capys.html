Gassy Capys

One-Tap Capybara Chaos

In Review

Free · Remove Ads one-time $0.99

The flappy formula, rebuilt around a capybara with a gas problem. One tap to fly. Dodge everything. Chase your best score. That's the whole game — and it's harder than it looks.

Features

  • One-tap arcade flying — anyone can learn it in five seconds; mastering it is another story
  • A soundtrack of the creator's own songs — cycling Damaged → Trapped → Wasted as you play
  • The 5-crash gate — after every 5th crash, watch a rewarded ad or wait 30 seconds; the unlock makes it instant
  • Free with ads — every feature is in the free game; the $0.99 unlock removes ads only
