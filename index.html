About

JaeForge is the studio of Elliott Hornick, a personal trainer and fitness coach in Fargo, North Dakota. Elliott lost 130 pounds on his own fitness journey before he ever coached anyone else — he knows exactly which tools he wished he'd had.

So he builds them: the camera remote he needed to film workouts solo, the boxing coach that fits in a pocket, the deck builder he wanted at the table. Every JaeForge app starts as a problem Elliott ran into himself — in the gym, on camera, or mid-game — and ends as a small, focused tool that solves it.
