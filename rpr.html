RPR: Roll Punch Repeat

Dice Boxing Combo Game

In Review

Free · “Lucky Punch — Remove Ads” one-time $9.99

RPR: Roll Punch Repeat is a boxing game where dice call the shots. Roll for a combo of 2–6 punches, then throw it — 10 reps a combo, fullscreen rounds, a round timer, breaks between combos, and speed you control from 1–15. The dice math means 55,980 possible combos, so the workout never runs out of new rounds.

Features

  • Dice-driven combos — the roll is the game, not a playlist; every round is a fresh draw from 55,980 combinations
  • Fullscreen rounds — combo and rep counters, countdown beeps, and breaks between combos that you set (1–3 seconds)
  • Speed you control — callout pace from 1 to 15, like a treadmill speed control
  • Challenges and Other Drills tabs — more ways to train beyond the main game
  • Music built in — the soundtrack is the creator's own songs
  • Free with ads, every feature included — the one-time unlock removes ads only. No feature gates, no subscription
