Privacy Policy — Elliott Hornick apps

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the iOS apps iControl Snap, RPR: Roll Punch Repeat, and Gassy Capys, published by Elliott Hornick (“we,” “the developer”). Contact: [email protected]

The short version

These apps have no user accounts and the developer runs no servers that receive your personal data. What you create in the apps stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

iControl Snap (camera app)

  • Photos and videos you record are saved to your device’s Photos library. They are never sent to the developer.
  • The app uses the camera, microphone, and Bluetooth (for controller input) only to provide its features, and only with your permission.
  • If you use a share or upload feature (for example, posting a video to YouTube or TikTok), the file goes directly from your device to that service under your own account, only when you tap to share it. Those services’ own privacy policies apply.
  • If you redeem a promo code, redemption is handled by Apple in the App Store; the app only learns whether the unlock is active.

RPR: Roll Punch Repeat and Gassy Capys (games)

  • Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • The games contain no accounts, no chat, and no location features.

Advertising (all three apps)

The free versions show ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP, and ad interaction data, in line with Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 Where iOS asks, tracking is only used with your permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can decline and you will still be able to use the apps (ads may be less relevant). Purchasing a Pro/unlock version removes ads.

Purchases

Pro unlocks and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment details. Manage or cancel via your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
