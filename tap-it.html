Tap It!

A roguelike TCG tournament where the cards are invented fresh every run.

In Development

In development · price to be announced

Tap It! is a tournament-simulator roguelike trading card game. Every run generates a brand-new card pool — influencers, actions, responses and Media Empires — and deals you a seed you can replay or share, Balatro-style.

Draft 1 of 3 Media Empires, let the draft build you a legal deck (15 influencers, 35+ main), then fight through an 8-player Swiss bracket of best-of-3 sets into a top-4 cut. Win a game by knocking out 7 of their influencers, emptying their influencer deck, or buying the vote at 100 cred. Lose a match and the run ends.

Every card's art is generated from its own seed — hand-drawn look, vintage neon-tube frames — so no two runs ever look or play the same.

Features

  • A fresh card pool every run — generated influencers, actions, responses and Media Empires; replay or share any run by its seed
  • Draft and go — pick 1 of 3 Media Empires and the draft builds a legal deck: 15 influencers, 35+ main
  • Real tournament structure — 8-player Swiss, best-of-3 sets, top-4 cut; lose a match and the run ends
  • Three ways to win a game — 7 influencer KOs, an emptied influencer deck, or buy the vote at 100 cred
  • Click-to-draw turns — and a response-chain system for fighting back on your opponent's turn
  • Media Empire tap abilities — once-per-game powers printed right on the empire
  • Tournament cheats — from the second tournament on, rivals start bending the rules
  • Tap any card to read it — its full text, big and clear, separate from the art
